Interactive sensing · 2024

TIME

An interactive 360° environment built in TouchDesigner. A webcam reads the viewer’s head, and turning it moves them through the artist’s homes in Tabriz, Hamedan and Philadelphia, rebuilt as one panoramic space with original music. The body becomes the interface for memory and displacement.

Interactive sensing · 2024

INTERACT

An interactive display built in TouchDesigner with webcam motion tracking. A century of images of Iranian women, drawn from Qajar-era photographs, moves at a speed set by the person watching, so the archive answers the one who looks at it.
