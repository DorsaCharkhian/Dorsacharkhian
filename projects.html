BFA thesis · Heritage research · 2022

Orosi: from historic windows to modern lighting

Historical and field research on Qajar-era orosi (sash windows) at the Hegmataneh Museum in Hamedan. I photographed and redrew their patterns, traced a shift from geometric to arabesque and naturalistic motifs, and explored how they could live on in contemporary wooden lighting.

Interactive machine learning · 2023

Teachable Machine experiments

Coursework at Drexel connecting Teachable Machine models to real-time interaction in TouchDesigner: an early exploration of training a model by example and using its output to drive an interactive piece.

Human-in-the-loop AI agents for trading decisions

A two-agent decision-support system for a small investment firm. One agent researches and proposes trades; an independent safety agent checks six hard risk rules, logs every decision and alerts the client, who makes every final call.
